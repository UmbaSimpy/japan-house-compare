# Child-rearing support in Tokyo's 23 wards + 9 Tama cities (Musashino, Mitaka, Chofu, Fuchu, Koganei, Tachikawa, Machida, Tama, Inagi). Research date 2026-10-12

Scope: a family with a child aged 0–2 who may have a second child. Every item notes its effective date. **"Cited"** means the fact came from a source fetched or searched in this pass. **"Inference"** means my own reasoning.

---

## Q1. What does a 0–2 child cost per month in 認可保育園 for a ¥10M dual-income household, after the Tokyo free programme?

### Takeaway
From **September 2025** the base 認可 保育料 for **every** 0–2 child is ¥0 in all 23 wards and all 9 Tama cities. This is true whatever the income or birth order. Two things make it so: Tokyo's 第1子 free programme (from 2025-09-01) and its earlier 第2子 free programme (from 2023-10). A ¥10M household now pays ¥0 base fee in every municipality on the list. What remains is extras the programme excludes: 延長保育料, diapers, supplies, event fees and uniforms. **The fee has stopped being a differentiator between municipalities. Getting a place (Q2) is now the real differentiator.**

### Cited Findings
- Tokyo began making 0–2 第1子 保育料 free on 2025-09-01 — [BCN Retail 2025-09-04](https://www.bcnretail.com/market/detail/20250904_554110.html).
- Scope in Suginami's ward council material:
  - Fees become free for 第1子 aged 0–2 in 住民税課税世帯 at 認可保育所 and 地域型保育事業, the cases that were previously paid in full by parents.
  - Separate subsidies apply to 認証保育所 and 認可外 fees.
  - [Suginami material r070609](https://www.city.suginami.tokyo.jp/documents/19521/r070609hokensiryou4.pdf)
- Nerima: from 2025-09-01 the 0–2 第1子 in taxed households (until then outside the national scheme) became free. With that, Nerima says **all children** are covered. Some facilities need paperwork or have subsidy caps. — [Nerima 第2子/第1子 無償化 page](https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kosodatekyoiku/kodomo/hoiku/dainishimusyouka.html)
- Koto: 第1子 保育料 free "年齢や課税状況にかかわらず" (regardless of age or tax status).
  - Covers the monthly 保育料 only.
  - **Excluded:** 月極/スポット延長保育料, daily goods, stationery, uniforms, **おむつ代**, event fees.
  - No application needed.
  - [Koto 第1子無償化](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/280308/daiissimusyouka.html); [Koto 減免](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/280308/kodomo/hoiku/ninka/genmen.html)
- Edogawa: from 2025-09 the base 保育料 at 認可 facilities is free.
  - 延長保育料 is excluded.
  - The household must still file 住民税 so the public cost can be calculated.
  - [Edogawa 利用者負担](https://www.city.edogawa.tokyo.jp/e048/kosodate/kosodate/hoiku/nitijou/hoikuen/riyoushahutan.html)
- Shinjuku: the basic 保育料 at 認可保育園 and 認定こども園 (保育園 function) has been fully publicly funded from the September 2025 fee — [Shinjuku hoiku02_002044](https://www.city.shinjuku.lg.jp/kodomo/hoiku02_002044.html).
- Earlier programme: Tokyo made the **第2子** 0–2 保育料 free, removing the national rule that the older sibling had to be in preschool at the same time. Effective October 2023 (date from the article's context; not re-verified on a primary page in this pass) — [financial-field](https://financial-field.com/living/entry-185510).
- Article comparing savings from the 第1子 free programme at household incomes of ¥4M, ¥6M and ¥8M (not fetched; listed as a lead) — [financial-field 403726](https://financial-field.com/living/entry-403726).
- Possible side effect: Tokyo's own Q&A on the April 2026 waitlist mentions the 2025-09 第1子 free programme. It calls the rise in demand "複合的な要因" (multiple factors combined) — [Tokyo 福祉局 Q&A](https://www.fukushi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kodomo/hoiku/hoikuriyoujyoukyou/qa2).

### Inferences
- For the target family (¥10M household, child aged 1 in 認可), the monthly 認可 cost is **¥0 base fee plus extras** in all 32 municipalities. Extras would typically be 延長保育 (a few thousand yen a month if used), diapers and supplies.
- I found no municipality on the list that keeps charging a 0–2 認可 base fee.
- **Counterfactual value (assumption, not sourced):** before September 2025, a ¥10M dual-income household's 0–2 認可 fee in the 23 wards was typically around ¥30,000–60,000/month, depending on the ward's fee table and each spouse's 住民税所得割. Treat this as an assumption range; the Q3 benefit estimate uses ¥45,000/month as the midpoint.
- 認証保育所 and 認可外: Tokyo also subsidises 第1子 fees there, but caps apply. If the family ends up in 認証 because no 認可 place is available, a net cost may remain (cap not verified, see Gaps).

### Gaps
- Exact pre-free fee tables for a ¥10M household in each of the 32 municipalities were not collected (no longer relevant to net cost).
- Tokyo's official page (fukushi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kodomo/hoiku/mushouka) was not fetched directly. The rule is confirmed via 5 ward pages and Suginami's council material.
- Monthly cap of the 認証保育所 第1子 subsidy: not verified.
- Ward-specific extras on top of free fees (e.g. free 延長保育, free diapers at daycare) were not surveyed systematically. Koto partially exempts 月極延長保育料 at ward-run nurseries.

## Q2. 待機児童 (Apr 2025 vs Apr 2026), 学童 waitlists, and the other items per municipality: which wards/cities stand out?

### Takeaway
- **Tokyo-wide waitlist:** **339 (2025-04-01) → 1,113 (2026-04-01)**, a 774 rise, the first big jump in years. The main daycare waitlist hotspots in 2026 are:
  - 世田谷 166
  - 足立 106
  - 北 103
  - 町田 90
  - 江戸川 75

  - Tama cities: 武蔵野, 三鷹

  - 葛飾 320

  - 大田 139
  - 中央 118
  - 多摩 105
  - 稲城 102
- **Medical:** all 32 cover to age 18 with no income limit. Copay is ¥0 everywhere except **町田, 小金井, 多摩, 稲城**, which charge ¥200 per outpatient visit from elementary school up. Preschoolers are ¥0 everywhere.

### Cited Findings: 子ども医療費 (medical-expense subsidy)
- Tokyo's マル子 (compulsory-school age) base rules:
  - Outpatient: up to ¥200 per visit; pharmacy dispensing and home nursing are free.
  - Inpatient: the 食事療養標準負担額 (standard meal charge) is payable.
  - Tokyo's page says copays vary by municipality and some charge none.
  - [Tokyo 福祉局 マル子](https://www.fukushi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/seikatsu/josei/maruko)
- Income limit: Tokyo removed it for マル乳, マル子 and マル青 from **2025-10** — [Fuchu/Fussa council material](https://www.city.fussa.tokyo.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/020/251/202506053-4.pdf).
  - Nikkei reported Tokyo's policy as making visits possible for "全員が無償を含め200円以下" (everyone at ¥200 or less, including free). **This caps the copay at ¥200; it does not abolish it.** Whether to drop the ¥200 is up to each municipality — [Nikkei](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOCC06BNI0W4A900C2000000/).
  - **So there was no Tokyo-wide "2025 copay abolition". The 2025 change was the income-limit removal.**
- 23 wards: free medical to age 18 (no copay, no income limit) since April 2023 — [Diamond](https://diamond.jp/articles/-/320426). The goo list shows all 23 wards as "18歳3月末まで / 自己負担なし / 所得制限なし".

### Cited Findings: 給食費 (school lunch)
- Tokyo subsidised half of municipalities' school-lunch costs from FY2024. A September 2024 supplementary budget of ¥1.7bn raised the effective rate for 市町村 to 7/8. From January 2025 every remaining municipality, including Koganei and Machida, made lunch free. So all 23 wards and all Tama 市町村 have free elementary and junior-high lunch:
  - [BCN Retail 2024-12-14](https://www.bcnretail.com/market/detail/20241214_476460.html)
  - [Akahata 2025-01-18](https://www.jcp.or.jp/akahata/aik24/2025-01-18/2025011802_01_0.html) (party paper; fact consistent with BCN)
  - [Musashimurayama council opinion](https://www.city.musashimurayama.lg.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/006/252/r6-1gakkoukyuusyoku.pdf)
- Funding split:
  - **Wards:** municipal plus Tokyo's 1/2.
  - **Tama cities:** municipal plus Tokyo up to about 7/8.
- National layer from April 2026: MEXT funds public **elementary** school lunch ingredients, capped at ¥5,200/month per pupil in FY2026. MEXT calls it 「負担軽減」 (burden reduction), not 「無償化」 (free provision) — [MEXT](https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/sports/syokuiku/kyu-lighten.html).
  - Setagaya says it continues free elementary and junior-high lunch — [Setagaya](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02056/1959.html).

  - 新宿 is near this group (学童 waitlist only 5).
  - 江東 and 葛飾: daycare waitlist 0, but 学童 waitlists are rising.
- **Negative standouts:**
  - **世田谷** (166, the largest in Japan)
  - **足立** (106) and **北** (103): both jumped from single or low double digits.
  - **町田**: daycare 90, plus the ¥200 copay.
  - **立川**: daycare 45, 学童 142.
  - **江戸川**: daycare 0 → 75.
  - **稲城/多摩**: 学童 waitlist around 10% of registrants, plus the ¥200 copay.
  - **杉並/葛飾**: worst 学童 in the 23 wards.

- The headline waitlist undercounts real difficulty. Families who decline offered places or are on 育休 extensions are excluded. Expect 0-歳 April entry to remain competitive in the low-waitlist wards too (inference).
- Medical copay only matters once children reach elementary age. For a 0–2 family it is ¥0 in all 32 municipalities.

### Gaps
- Official 入園しやすさ metrics (入園内定率, 指数 cut-offs) were not collected per municipality.
- 2026 学童 numbers are preliminary (速報).
- The medical-copay list (goo) is undated. 府中, 武蔵野, 三鷹 and 町田 were confirmed on official or council pages. 小金井, 多摩, 稲城, 調布 and 立川 were not checked against their official sites in this pass; 2025-10 changes may not be reflected.
- Tokyo's FY2026 continuation and subsidy rate for junior-high 給食費 was not found in a primary source. All 32 municipalities were free from January 2025, and nothing found indicates a reversal.

## Q3. Cash benefits and other perks, and the yen-valued annual benefit for a family with a 1-year-old and a newborn

### Takeaway
At the Tokyo level, a family with a 1-year-old and a 2026 newborn receives:
- **¥120,000/yr** in 018サポート
- a one-off **¥130,000** 赤ちゃんファースト(+) gift for the newborn

On top of that come national cash and avoided daycare fees. Adding everything up gives roughly **¥0.6M–1.6M in year 1** depending on daycare use (estimate below). Ward extras matter most for **港区** (up to ¥310,000 extra delivery subsidy) and possibly **千代田区**; most Tama cities on the list add little or nothing in cash.

### Cited Findings: Tokyo-level
- **018サポート:**
  - ¥5,000/month (up to ¥60,000/yr) per child aged 0–18 living in Tokyo, no income limit.
  - FY2026 application window: 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.
  - [Tokyo 福祉局 018 leaflet](https://www.fukushi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/documents/d/fukushi/2026-07-02-093829-831)
  - Other Tokyo 福祉局 documents: [2026-06-29](https://www.fukushi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/documents/d/fukushi/2026-06-29-143411-089); [2026-07-28](https://www.fukushi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/documents/d/fukushi/2026-07-28-144839-423)
- **赤ちゃんファースト:**
  - Gift-card equivalent of ¥100,000 for births from 2025-04-01 to 2025-12-31.
  - **¥130,000 for births from 2026-01-01 to 2027-03-31.** This is the ¥100,000 gift plus a new ¥30,000 "赤ちゃんファースト＋".
  - Can be applied for together with 018サポート; delivered about 4 months after application.
  - Same Tokyo 福祉局 sources as above.
- **Tokyo FY2025 announcements:** [Tokyo press 2025-03-28](https://www.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/information/press/2025/03/2025032852); [attachment](https://www.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/documents/d/tosei/20250328_47_06).
- **National (for context):**
  - **妊婦支援給付金:** ¥50,000 after the pregnancy-notification interview plus ¥50,000 per child after birth, made permanent from April 2025.
  - **出産育児一時金:** ¥500,000.
  - Both as reported in an aggregator summary — [hoken-minaoshi-lab](https://www.hoken-minaoshi-lab.jp/article/tokyo_child_support/); [hojyokin-concierge](https://hojyokin-concierge.com/media/2024/05/23/news_toukyo_23_shuxtusan_jyoseikin_2024).

### Cited Findings: ward/city-specific cash (mostly aggregators; amounts conflict, flagged)
- **港区 出産費用助成:** reimburses actual delivery costs above the ¥500,000 national lump sum, up to a ¥810,000 cap. **The maximum extra is ¥310,000.** Another article's "60万円" figure looks outdated.
  - [hojyokin-concierge](https://hojyokin-concierge.com/media/2024/05/23/news_toukyo_23_shuxtusan_jyoseikin_2024); [machikurabe 2026-04-14](https://machikurabe.jp/guide/tokyo-kosodate-ranking)
  - 港区 also gives a 育児パッケージ of taxi vouchers and goods vouchers (amount not confirmed).
- **千代田区:**
  - Delivery subsidy reportedly up to ¥310,000.
  - **誕生準備手当: conflicting amounts.** One source says a flat ¥45,000 after week 20 of pregnancy; machikurabe says ¥450,000.
  - **次世代育成手当:** ¥15,000/month according to machikurabe and dr-asset. Age eligibility was not verified; historically it targeted the 15–18 age band (unverified).

### Annual benefit estimate (worked example)

Family assumptions:
- Child A aged 1, 第1子, in 認可.
- Child B born in 2026 (第2子), enters 認可 at around age 0–1.
- Household income ¥10M, both parents working.
- Uses no ward extras unless stated.

**Year 1** (12 months after child B's birth):

### Inferences
- Fees, medical and lunch are now nearly uniform across Tokyo. **The real municipal differentiators for this family are:**
  - (a) the 0–2 daycare and 学童 waitlist;
  - (b) one-off ward cash in 港, 渋谷 and 千代田;
  - (c) the ¥200 copay in 町田, 小金井, 多摩 and 稲城 (only from elementary school; at about 10 visits/yr per child that is around ¥2,000/child/yr, so negligible).
- The roughly ¥1M/yr avoided-fee value is the single biggest number. It applies everywhere in Tokyo, **but only if a 認可 place is obtained.** In 世田谷, 足立 or 北, failing to get a 0–1 place and paying 認証 or 認可外 with a capped subsidy could erase part of it.

### Gaps (other perks, not researched or not found)
- **おむつ定期便:** no reliable per-ward list found. Several wards (e.g. 文京 and 世田谷-type 見守り programmes) are rumoured but **unverified**.
- **一時保育:** fees and subsidies per municipality were not collected. Tokyo has expanded subsidies for 定期利用 and 一時預かり, but no figures were gathered.
- **第2子 rent / housing support:** none found in this pass. Some wards (e.g. 新宿 or 千代田 次世代 housing) have historically offered family relocation or rent subsidies; unverified.
- **School-entry money (入学祝い金):** not found for any of the 32 municipalities in the sources reviewed.
- **Changes during 2026:** found 赤ちゃんファースト+ (+¥30,000, births from 2026-01-01), the national elementary lunch support (2026-04) and the waitlist jump. Any FY2027 changes to 018サポート (e.g. amount increase) were not found.
- **千代田区:** 誕生準備手当 and 次世代育成手当 amounts and ages need checking on the official ward site because aggregators conflict.
