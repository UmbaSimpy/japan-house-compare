> **How to read these notes.** The "latest population" column uses the **2025 national census (reference date 2025-10-01)**, not the resident registry. The 2025 census is newer, sets the base for every monthly prefectural estimate from now on, and covers all candidates on one basis. Its preliminary (速報) results came out 2026-05-29 and the final count (確報, 人口等基本集計) on 2026-09-29. The master table below uses the uub.jp compilation. Its 2025 figures match the 確報 (e.g. Nagareyama 215,086 = 199,849 + 15,237, the 確報 change Chiba published), so they are 1–200 persons off the 速報 numbers that prefectures printed in May. The 2015 and 2020 values are census counts derived from uub.jp's 5- and 10-year change columns. **IPSS publishes ward-level projections for 政令市 wards (Chiba, Saitama, Kawasaki, Yokohama), so ward-level data is used throughout and no fallback to city level was needed.**

## Q1. Which municipalities are growing, and which are shrinking? (census 2015→2020→2025, IPSS 2023 projection to 2035/2050)

### Takeaway
Growth is concentrated in three places: (a) central and east Tokyo (中央・台東・江東・墨田・港・文京), (b) the TX/北総 corridor (つくば +11.2% 2020→25, 流山 +7.6%, 印西 +7.5%, つくばみらい +4.5%), and (c) a few station-redevelopment wards (千葉市中央区 +7.2%, 美浜区 +4.9%, さいたま市大宮区 +6.4%, 川崎市多摩区 +3.2%). The clearest decliners are old new-town and outer areas: 多摩市 −3.7%, 市原市 −3.6%, 佐倉市 −3.0%, 町田市 −2.5%, 白井市 −2.4%, 横浜市青葉区 −1.3%, さいたま市岩槻区 −1.4%. Several "family suburbs" that grew in 2015–20 have now flattened or turned negative: 戸田 −1.2%, 三郷 −1.1%, 川口 −0.4%, 市川 −0.5%, 松戸 −0.4%, 習志野 −0.3%, 三鷹 −0.3%. To 2050, IPSS projects only 流山 (+20.9%) and 印西 (+16.8%) among the Chiba suburbs, and つくばみらい (+13.0%), 守谷 (+11.4%) and つくば (+6.0%) in Ibaraki, to grow strongly. Most Chiba-shi wards, 佐倉 and 市原 are projected to lose 14–23%.

## Q2. Which have major pipeline projects that will improve access or amenities (through ~2030 and beyond)?

### Takeaway
Few big access upgrades will actually open by 2030. Those that will: the 羽田空港アクセス線 (東山手ルート, target FY2031, i.e. by 2032-03), the TX 8-car upgrade (early 2030s), and the already-open 相鉄・東急新横浜線 (2023-03). The two Tokyo Metro extensions (有楽町線 豊洲–住吉 and 南北線 品川–白金高輪) are under construction for the mid-2030s. TX to 土浦/東京, 地下鉄8号線 to 野田市, 埼玉高速 to 岩槻, 多摩モノレール to 町田, ブルーライン to 新百合ヶ丘 and 新空港線 (2038–42) are all planning-stage or long-dated. On the amenity side, the bigger items are 高輪ゲートウェイシティ (opened 2025-03), 晴海フラッグ (about 11,000 residents), and Chiba-shi central/Makuhari redevelopment. Two flagship projects have stalled because of construction costs: 津田沼南口 and 中野サンプラザ.

The 江東 有楽町線 and 港 品川 extensions are real but arrive mid-2030s. Everything else (8号線 to 野田, TX to 土浦/東京, 岩槻, 町田 monorail, ブルーライン, 蒲蒲線) should be treated as optional upside after 2035.
- Condo supply is shifting outward to 都下 and back into 千葉 in 2026. 23区 prices (about ¥136m average) are pushing families to 足立/北/大田 and to 都下, 千葉 and さいたま. The HRI and LIFULL commentary both describe this.

- Whether a 2025 交通政策審議会 statement changed the status of 8号線 to 野田 could not be confirmed.

## Q3. Which have high or rising child populations (a family-friendliness proxy)?

### Takeaway
By 0–14 share in the 2020 census, the leaders among candidates are 印西 (16.4%), 流山 (15.6%), さいたま市緑区 (15.1%), 守谷 (15.1%), 横浜市都筑区 (15.0%), つくばみらい (14.7%), つくば (14.3%), 戸田 (14.2%), 稲城 (14.1%) and 千葉市緑区 (14.0%). In April 2025 Chiba's own registry ranked 印西 (16.5%) and 流山 (16.2%) as the top two in the prefecture, with the lowest average ages after 浦安. The central wards 中央・千代田・港 are surprisingly high (about 13.3–13.7%). The lowest are 新宿・中野・豊島・台東・渋谷 (8.4–9.4%). Net migration shows families leaving the 23 wards as a whole: children aged 14 and under are in net outflow, and families 35+ are leaving with them. The beneficiaries are 足立/北/大田 inside Tokyo, and outside it 流山, つくば, 千葉市中央区, さいたま市緑区, 八千代, 船橋 and 町田. Kisarazu is not a standout on these numbers.

### Inferences
- **Best combined "family" signal** (high child share, still growing in 2020–25, projected growth to 2050, and positive net migration in 2025): 流山, 印西, つくば, つくばみらい, 守谷, さいたま市緑区/浦和区/南区, 川崎市幸区/中原区, 稲城, and 江東区 within the 23 wards (12.7% child share, +5.6%).
- **High child share but stalling:** 戸田 (14.2%, −1.2%), 横浜市都筑区 (15.0%, +0.4%), 千葉市緑区 (14.0%, −1.0%), 白井 (13.8%, −2.4%), 袖ケ浦 (13.5% and rising to 13.6% in 2025, +1.9%). These may be past their peak family-inflow phase as the first cohorts of children age out.
- 浦安 has a young adult structure (lowest elderly share and average age in Chiba) but only average child share and flat population. IPSS projects −4.4% by 2050.
- There is no ward- or city-level 0–14 or 25–44 net-migration figure for 2025 in free press material. The patterns above come from total net migration plus age-structure snapshots.
