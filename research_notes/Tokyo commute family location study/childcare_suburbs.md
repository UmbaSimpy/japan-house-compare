# Child-rearing support in suburban candidate municipalities (Chiba / Ibaraki / Saitama / Kanagawa), compared with Tokyo

Research date: 2026-10-12. The figures are as of fiscal 2025–2026, and each one gives its effective date.

**Household assumption used everywhere below ("¥10M dual income"):** the household has two salaried earners, e.g. ¥6M + ¥4M or ¥5M + ¥5M. Social insurance is about 14.5% and only basic deductions apply; children under 16 give no 扶養控除.
- ¥6M earner: 給与所得 = ¥6,000,000 − ¥1,640,000 = ¥4.36M. Subtract 社保 ¥0.87M and 基礎控除 (住民税) ¥0.43M to get ¥3.06M. ×6% = ¥183,600, less 調整控除 ¥1,500 → **¥182,100**.
- ¥4M earner: ¥4,000,000 − ¥1,280,000 = ¥2.72M. Subtract ¥0.58M and ¥0.43M to get ¥1.71M. ×6% = ¥102,600, less ¥1,500 → **¥101,100**.
- **The combined 市町村民税所得割 is about ¥283,000. A 5M+5M split gives about ¥285,600.** I use about ¥285,000 throughout.
- Fees use 所得割 **before** 住宅ローン控除 and ふるさと納税, so a mortgage does not lower the fee. 政令市 recompute their 8% tax at the 6% rate. Chiba-shi, Kawasaki and Saitama-shi say this explicitly, and Nagareyama does the same for parents taxed in a 政令市.
- **Sensitivity:** about ¥285k sits just under the common ¥301,000 bracket edge. If 所得割 goes above ¥301k (e.g. single earner, or ¥11M+ dual), most cities move one bracket up, adding about ¥3–5k/month. Older articles that assume ¥10M household income often use about ¥376,600 所得割, which is a **single** earner, and so overstate the dual-income fee.
- Fees for April–August use the previous year's tax and fees for September–March use the current year's. The fee is set by **age class at 4/1**, and 0, 1 and 2-year-olds pay the same "3歳未満" price.

## Q1. What does a 0–2 child cost per month in 認可保育園 for a ¥10M dual-income household in each municipality, and what free / multi-child programmes exist beyond the national rule?

### Takeaway
In most candidate cities the first child aged 0–2 costs **about ¥45,000–61,000/month** (保育標準時間). Tsukuba is cheapest among the confirmed paying cities at ¥45,000. Chiba-shi, Funabashi and Saitama-shi are about ¥54–55k, and Nagareyama, Kawaguchi, Soka, Yokohama and Kawasaki are about ¥60–60.5k. Two Chiba cities have fully removed 0–2 fees, matching Tokyo: **Urayasu, free for all 0–2 since 2026-04-01**, and **Ichikawa, first child 0–2 free since 2026-09, no income limit**. The premise that Chiba, Ibaraki and Saitama run prefectural "第2子 0–2 free" programmes was **not confirmed**. Saitama's prefecture-wide scheme covers **第3子以降** only. Ibaraki's prefectural subsidy covers 第3子+, plus 第2子 for incomes of about ¥3.6–6.4M only, so it does not apply to a ¥10M household. No Chiba prefectural 0–2 fee programme was found.

### Cited Findings

**Prefectural programmes (premise check)**
- Saitama Prefecture runs a scheme jointly with municipalities. It makes fees free for **満3歳未満の第3子以降**, regardless of the eldest child's age. 第2子 is not covered. In 2023 Governor Ono answered that 第2子 free childcare should be done nationally by the state. — [埼玉県 多子世帯への保育料支援](https://www.pref.saitama.lg.jp/b0616/hoiku-jinzai/tashisetai.html); [埼玉県議会 R5.6 一般質問](https://www.pref.saitama.lg.jp/e1601/gikai-gaiyou/r0506/4/e0110.html)
- In Soka, the prefectural 3rd-child scheme makes 0–2 第3子以降 free regardless of income, but it needs an application (草加市多子世帯保育料軽減申請書). — [草加市 保育所等の利用者負担額](https://www.city.soka.saitama.jp/cont/s1603/020/010/010/02.html)
- Ibaraki Prefecture's 多子世帯保育料軽減事業費補助 covers two groups. The first is 第3子以降 under 3, with no income limit. The second is 第2子 under 3 in households at national 第4–5階層, about ¥3.6–6.4M income. The prefecture and municipality each pay half. The source is a FY2019 document, and FY2022 shows the same structure. — [茨城県 補助制度資料 (35hukushi.pdf)](https://www.pref.ibaraki.jp/somu/shichoson/zaisei/joseiseido/documents/35hukushi.pdf)
- No Chiba Prefecture 0–2 fee programme was found in searches, including FY2026 budget terms. Chiba Prefecture's visible prefectural programme is **第3子以降 school-lunch free**. — [千葉県 学校給食費無償化一覧 R7.9.1](https://www.pref.chiba.lg.jp/kyouiku/anzen/kyuushoku/gakkoukyuushoku/documents/mushoukaichiran.pdf)

## Q2. How does this compare with Tokyo (第1子 0–2 free from 2025-09; 018サポート ¥5,000/month)?

### Takeaway
On cash value, Tokyo beats every suburb except Urayasu and Ichikawa (from 2026-09) by **about ¥650–730k/year on daycare fees alone for one child aged 0–2**. Add **¥60k/year per child** (018サポート) and a **¥100–130k one-off** birth gift card. Urayasu and Ichikawa now match Tokyo on 0–2 fees. They still lack 018サポート (−¥60k/child/year) and the 赤ちゃんファースト gift.

### Cited Findings
- **Tokyo daycare:** 第1子 0–2 free from 令和7年9月以降, "国が実施するまでの間", with no income limit. Together with 第2子 (2023-10) and 第3子+ (2019-10), this makes daycare free for every child in Tokyo. — [東京都の少子化対策2025 p.30](https://www.spt.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kodomoseisaku/book/2025-02_tokyo_syoshika2025/pageindices/index30.html); [東京動画](https://tokyodouga.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/bdk3nx5b0bi.html)
- **018サポート:** ¥5,000/month (¥60,000/year) for every child aged 0–18 living in Tokyo, with no income limit. — [Impress Watch](https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1599115.html); [東京都 press 2025-03-28](https://www.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/information/press/2025/03/2025032852)
- **赤ちゃんファースト (東京都出産・子育て応援事業):** a gift card worth ¥100,000 for births 2025-04-01 to 2025-12-31, and **¥130,000 for births 2026-01-01 to 2027-03-31**. — [練馬区 事業案内](https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kosodatekyoiku/shussan/20230110.files/jigyouannnai.pdf)
- **Tokyo 23-ward school lunch:** all 23 wards made public elementary and junior-high lunch free (from FY2023) with Tokyo-metro subsidy. This is a non-official aggregator. — [machikurabe](https://machikurabe.jp/guide/kyushoku-muryou-jichitai-ichiran)
- **Tokyo 学童:** several wards have large waitlists, e.g. 杉並区 481, 中央区 275, 目黒区 246, 葛飾区 227, 足立区 179, 大田区 155 (2025-05-01). — [こども家庭庁 R7 放課後児童クラブ](https://www.cfa.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/69799c33-85cb-44f6-8c70-08ed3a292ab5/c253a682/20251222_policies_kosodateshien_houkago-jidou_80.pdf)
- **Suburban nursery fees:** see the Q1 table. Annual first-child fees outside the free cities are about ¥540k (Tsukuba) to about ¥726k (Kawasaki).

## Q3. Explicit yen-valued annual benefit for a family with one child aged 1 and one newborn

### Takeaway
Under the household assumptions above, municipal choice is worth **about ¥0.65–0.73M in the newborn year and about ¥0.8–1.09M/year once both children are in daycare**, for the cities that charge fees, compared with Urayasu, Ichikawa (from 2026-09) or Tokyo. Tokyo adds about ¥120k/year (018 ×2) and a ¥130k gift card on top. National benefits are the same everywhere: 児童手当 about ¥360k/year for two under-3s, 妊婦のための支援給付 ¥100k per birth, and 出産育児一時金 ¥500k.

### Cited Findings
- 児童手当: ¥15,000/month for under-3s (第3子以降 ¥30,000), ¥10,000 from age 3 to the end of high-school age. No income limit since the October 2024 benefit month. Paid every even month. — [こども家庭庁 もっと子育て応援！児童手当](https://www.cfa.go.jp/policies/kokoseido/jidouteate/mottoouen); [児童手当制度のご案内](https://www.cfa.go.jp/policies/kokoseido/jidouteate/annai)

- 出産育児一時金: ¥500,000 per child, or ¥488,000 at non-産科医療補償 facilities. — [流山市 出産育児一時金](https://www.city.nagareyama.chiba.jp/life/1000547/1000549/1000564.html)
- National elementary school-lunch support from 2026-04: ¥5,200/month standard for public elementary schools (¥6,200 for 特別支援), no income limit, no application, shared 1/2 by the state and 1/2 by the prefecture. Parents may still pay any excess over the standard. — [リセマム 2026-04-02](https://resemom.jp/article/2026/04/02/85681.html); [日経: 自維公合意](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUA153830V11C25A2000000/); [文科省 学校給食費の抜本的な負担軽減](https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/sports/syokuiku/kyu-lighten.html)
- Municipal cash birth bonuses: none were found for Urayasu, Nagareyama or Inzai. Their official pages show only the national 妊婦のための支援給付 and 出産育児一時金. A third-party claim of an Urayasu 第3子 ¥300k bonus was not officially confirmed. — [浦安市 妊婦のための支援給付](https://www.city.urayasu.lg.jp/kodomo/ninshin/1039002.html); [印西市](https://www.city.inzai.lg.jp/kosodatenavi/0000016542.html); [machikurabe 船橋 (non-official)](https://machikurabe.jp/guide/funabashi-shussan-iwaikin)
- Fees are from the Q1 table: Chiba-shi, Funabashi, Nagareyama, Tsukuba, Saitama-shi, Kawaguchi, Soka, Yokohama and Kawasaki.

### Inferences — worked estimate (all ¥, per year)

**Scenario assumptions:**
- Child A is 1 (1歳児クラス, in 認可, 保育標準時間) and child B is a newborn.
- **Year 1:** B is at home on 育休 and A stays enrolled (most cities allow continuation during 育休, sometimes as 短時間 at about ¥0.7–1.1k/month less).
- **Year 2:** A is in the 2歳児 class and B in the 0歳児 class.
- Household 所得割 is about ¥285k.
- Both children are counted under the national sibling rule, so B pays the 第2子 rate.
- 延長保育, 実費 and 給食 extras are excluded.

| Municipality | Yr-1 daycare (A only) | Yr-2 daycare (A + B) | Tokyo-only cash (018 ×2 + gift) | Yr-2 total "cost vs Tokyo" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tokyo 23区 | 0 | 0 | +120,000/yr (+130,000 one-off) | baseline |

| Yokohama | 720,000 | 1,068,000 (60,000 + 29,000) | – | about 1,188,000 |
| Nagareyama | 718,800 | 1,078,200 (59,900 + 29,950) | – | about 1,198,000 |
| Kawaguchi / Soka | 720,000 | 1,080,000 (60,000 + 30,000) | – | about 1,200,000 |

- **National benefits, the same in every municipality:**
  - 児童手当: A and B under 3 → 2 × ¥15,000 × 12 = **¥360,000/yr**.
  - 妊婦のための支援給付: **¥100,000** one-off for B.
  - 出産育児一時金: **¥500,000** one-off, which offsets the birth cost.

  - Child-medical copay at about 12 outpatient visits per child per year is ¥0 (Urayasu, Yokohama, Tokyo), ¥2,400 (¥200 cities), ¥3,600 (¥300 cities) or up to ¥7,200 (Ibaraki ¥600) per child per year.

- Whether a national 0–2 free programme is planned (which would end Tokyo's interim scheme and change all numbers) was not checked.

## Q4. Which municipalities are known for child-rearing (Nagareyama, Inzai, Tsukuba; "Akashi-style")?

### Takeaway
**Nagareyama**, **Inzai** and **Tsukuba** are the standout "family-magnet" suburbs by population growth. Nagareyama is known for its 送迎保育ステーション, Inzai for its long-running "住みよさ" top ranking in Chiba, and Tsukuba for the fastest population growth nationally among cities of 10k+. On hard yen, **Urayasu**, with 0–2 free plus free lunch through junior high and ¥0 medical copay, and **Ichikawa**, with 0–2 free from 2026-09 and free lunch through junior high, are now the most generous of the candidates. Ichikawa's weak point is its 学童 waitlist (283).

### Cited Findings
- **Population growth 2020→2025** (令和7年国勢調査 速報, municipalities with ≥10k people in 2020): 1st Tsukuba +11.3%, 3rd Nagareyama +7.6%, 4th Inzai +7.6%. — [東洋経済オンライン](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/950302?display=b), via search summary
- Nagareyama was marketed as "人口増加率6年連続1位" (2016–2021) in a publisher's promotion. — [財経新聞 / PR](https://www.zaikei.co.jp/releases/3313751/)
- **Nagareyama 送迎保育ステーション:** started in 2007, two years after the Tsukuba Express opened. Parents drop children at a station-front hub and buses take them to designated nurseries. The service now runs **8 buses** and is open to every resident child aged 1–5. — [文春オンライン](https://bunshun.jp/articles/-/60126)
- Nagareyama's nursery waitlist has been 0 since 2023 (R5) and the hidden waitlist is 81. — [Chiba Pref. R8 list](https://www.pref.chiba.lg.jp/jika/hoikusho/jouhou/documents/r080401itiran.pdf); [CFA R7 Excel](https://www.cfa.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/b0a8057b-34bf-4c20-84fb-ae592708ca9b/1aa96453/20250828_policies_hoiku_torimatome_r7_04.xlsx)
- **Inzai:** the city reports being 千葉県 No.1 in the 2025 東洋経済「住みよさランキング」. Earlier editions had it at the top for many years, including 7 consecutive years as of 2018. Its school lunch has been free for all since 2024-09. — [千葉日報/PR TIMES](https://www.chibanippo.co.jp/prtimes/1638491); [東洋経済 2018](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/225720); [印西市 給食費無償化](https://www.city.inzai.lg.jp/0000018326.html)
- **Tsukuba:** nursery capacity grew by +397 places in 2025 (資料1-1, municipalities with ≥100 increase). Its waitlist was 0 in both 2024 and 2025. — [CFA R7 資料1〜6 Excel](https://www.cfa.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/b0a8057b-34bf-4c20-84fb-ae592708ca9b/1a728dcc/20250828_policies_hoiku_torimatome_r7_02.xlsx)
- **Urayasu:** 0–2 fees free from 2026-04 with no conditions, all school lunch free since FY2024, and child medical copay ¥0 through high-school age. — [浦安市](https://www.city.urayasu.lg.jp/kodomo/hoiku/hoikuen/1047820.html); [PR TIMES (7市要請, 浦安市長発言)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000127.000133445.html); [Chiba Pref. medical list](https://www.pref.chiba.lg.jp/jika/boshi/kodomo-iryo/documents/joseinaiyou.pdf)

- **Seven Chiba cities, including Urayasu and Ichikawa, jointly asked the national government** to fund school-lunch free provision. — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000127.000133445.html)

### Inferences
- "Famous for child-rearing" (Nagareyama, Inzai, Tsukuba) is mostly about **growth, new housing, nursery capacity and station-area convenience**, not the lowest fees. Nagareyama's 0–2 fee (¥59,900 at the assumed income) is among the **highest** of the confirmed cities.
- The most "Akashi-like" candidates are Urayasu and Ichikawa. Akashi's package is known for free 0–2 daycare from the second child, free lunch, free medical and diaper delivery. Urayasu and Ichikawa now offer free 0–2 daycare, free junior-high lunch and medical support through high-school age.

### Gaps
- No sources were fetched on Akashi's own programme, so the comparison is descriptive only and Akashi's current yen values should be verified separately.
- 東洋経済 2025 住みよさ ranks for the other candidates were not retrieved.
- Nagareyama's 送迎保育ステーション fee (per use or monthly) was not retrieved.
- Other notable perks were not systematically surveyed for every city: diaper subscriptions (e.g. おむつ定期便), postpartum care subsidies, 一時預かり vouchers and the like.
