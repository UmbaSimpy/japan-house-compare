# Commuter highway buses into Tokyo Station / Otemachi / Nihonbashi (research date 2026-10-12)

Scope: weekday highway and express buses from suburbs in Chiba, Ibaraki, Saitama, Kanagawa and Tama that a commuter could use to reach the Tokyo Station area. Unit: municipality (市, or 区 of a designated city). The timetable date is flagged for each route. Counts come from the operators' own timetable pages unless noted otherwise.

Tokyo-side terminals used below:
- **BT東京八重洲 地下B**: the Aqua-Line routes, Sodegaura, Shirako and Katsuura. Bays B11–B13.
- **BT東京八重洲 地下A**: the Choshi lines and the Seaside Liner (A02/A03), since 2026-03-20.
- **東京駅日本橋口 (arrival) / JR高速バスのりば (departure)**: the Keisei inland Chiba routes and つくば号.

All of these are at Tokyo Station. Otemachi is a 5–10 minute walk from the Nihonbashi exit or Yaesu. No route terminates in Nihonbashi or Otemachi itself.

---

## Q1. Which origins have frequent commuter service? (per-municipality route table)

### Takeaway
Only the Aqua-Line corridor has frequent service: 木更津市 and 君津市, with 袖ケ浦市 a distant third. From 木更津金田BT about 26 buses leave for Tokyo between 05:00 and 07:59 on weekdays. Kimitsu adds 12 departures in the same window, plus 23 return buses from Tokyo between 17:00 and 21:30. Commuter passes cost ¥50,400–64,800 a month.

Every other candidate either has only 1–5 morning buses or has no Tokyo Station bus at all:
- 1–5 morning buses: 山武市/東金市 (Seaside Liner), 佐倉市 (Yukarigaoka), 千葉市若葉区/花見川区, 富里市, 大網白里市, 市原市 (鶴舞 only).
- No Tokyo Station bus found: 幕張 (Mihama/Hanamigawa), 千葉ニュータウン (印西/白井), 鎌ケ谷, 成田ニュータウン, 茂原 (suspended 2020), 市原 Anesaki/Goi, and 守谷.
- つくば市 is the one Ibaraki exception, with about 30 buses a day each way. Its morning ride is slow (85–105 min).

- 木更津～東京線, weekday: 9 departures from 木更津駅西口 reach BT東京八重洲 by 9:29 (5:00 → 6:04 … 8:15 → 9:29). Return departures from Yaesu run every 30 min, 17:30–21:30. Fare from 駅西口 is ¥1,500. ### Inferences
- **Frequent commuter service (≥ 9 Tokyo arrivals before 9:30): only 木更津市 and 君津市.** The 金田BT P&R hub adds about 26 departures from 05:00 to 07:59.
- **Moderate (3–5): 袖ケ浦市 and 山武市/東金市.**

## Q4. Realistic door-to-door: bus vs best rail (Tokyo Stn/Otemachi)

### Takeaway
Aqua-Line buses beat rail by a wide margin from 木更津/袖ケ浦/君津 in scheduled time: 64–74 min from 木更津駅 vs ~82 min by rail (2018). Add 10–25 min of morning Aqua-Line congestion and they draw roughly even, but the bus stays seated and direct.

Everywhere else (つくば, 佐倉, Makuhari, 千葉市) rail is faster: TX, Keisei/Sobu, or Keiyo. Buses there are a seated, slower alternative with 1–2 runs.

### Cited Findings
- Kisarazu rail at the weekday 7 a.m. hour: 8 trains (3 terminating at Chiba), ~1 h 22 min to Tokyo, ¥1,317 IC. Bus from 金田: ~40 min, ¥1,250 (2018) — [trafficnews](https://trafficnews.jp/post/80200)
- Scheduled bus rides in 2026:
  - 木更津駅西口 → Yaesu 64–74 min ([日東交通](https://www.nitto-kotsu.co.jp/at-%e6%9c%a8%e6%9b%b4%e6%b4%a5%e6%9d%b1%e4%ba%ac%e7%b7%9a%ef%bc%882026-1-01%ef%bd%9e%ef%bc%89/))
  - 袖ケ浦BT → Tokyo 50 min, plus 10–25 min possible delay ([小湊](https://www.kominato-bus.com/highway/high/sodegaura-tokyo.html))
  - つくば → Tokyo 85–105 min ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/つくば号))
  - ユーカリが丘 → Tokyo ~77–107 min ([京成](https://www.keiseibus.co.jp/express-bus/90404-2/))
  - 成東 → Tokyo 1 h 20 min – 2 h ([京成](https://www.keiseibus.co.jp/express-bus/90406-2/))
