# Costco Japan warehouses in greater Tokyo / Kanto, and nearest Costco per candidate municipality (as of Oct 2026)

## Which Costco warehouses exist in Kanto, and which openings are planned through 2027?

### Takeaway
Kanto has **14 operating Costco warehouses**: Tokyo 1, Kanagawa 3, Chiba 3, Saitama 2, Ibaraki 2, Tochigi 1, Gunma 2. Japan has 37 in total. **No new Kanto warehouse is officially announced or under construction for 2025–2027.** The only Japan opening in 2025 was 南アルプス (Yamanashi), and trackers list none in 2026. Several names in the brief are not real Costco warehouses: 守谷, 浦和美園 and 鶴ヶ島. The Saitama-city, Funabashi and Togane stores are only blog or forum rumours.

**Names in the brief that are not Costco warehouses**
- 守谷, 浦和美園 and 鶴ヶ島 do not appear in any current list: not Wikipedia, hubmedia, setu or OSM. Trackers mention no plans for them either — [kenbox](https://kenbox.jp/costco-newopen/); [matsukiyo](https://matsukiyo.cafe/costco-open/)

### Inferences
- **No change through 2027.** Japanese warehouses usually take about 2+ years from public reports to opening, and no Kanto site has reached even the report stage. The 14-store Kanto map is therefore very likely unchanged through end-2027. Any Saitama-city store would be 2028 at the earliest.
- **No Costco in the 23 wards.** The closest stores for central Tokyo are on its edges: 川崎 (south), 新三郷 (north-east) and 幕張 (east).

### Gaps
- **Official addresses and coordinates.** costco.co.jp's store finder is rendered with JavaScript and could not be fetched, so the official addresses could not be confirmed directly. They come from two aggregators that agree with each other.
- **Store matching in OSM.** The OSM nodes for ひたちなか, 群馬明和 and 金沢シーサイド have no branch tag. I matched them to stores by location; this is consistent with the addresses.
- **Mibu coordinates.** These come from the store's gas station node, about 100–300 m from the building.
- **Meiwa opening date.** Hubmedia calls Meiwa "opened last year" (i.e. 2025), but Wikipedia says 2023-04-26. Wikipedia is likely right: setu.co.jp already listed Meiwa in November 2023.
- **Hubmedia's 2026 opening.** Hubmedia says one store opened in 2026 but does not name it, and no other source (including matsukiyo, updated 2026-10-11) supports this. I treat it as an error.

## For each candidate municipality, which Costco is nearest and how far is the drive?

### Takeaway
- **Best access (10–25 min):** areas close to a warehouse.
  - Chiba: Mihama, Hanamigawa, Inage, Narashino, Funabashi (幕張); Inzai, Shiroi (千葉ニュータウン); Kisarazu, Sodegaura (木更津).
  - Saitama: Misato, Yashio, Soka, Koshigaya, plus Chiba's Nagareyama (新三郷).
  - Ibaraki: Tsukuba (つくば).
  - Kanagawa: Kawasaki-ku, Saiwai (川崎); Kanazawa-ku (金沢シーサイド).
  - Tokyo: Ota (川崎); Machida, Tama (多摩境/座間).
- **Weakest access (roughly 50–100 min):** central and western 23 wards (Nakano, Suginami, Nerima, Toshima, Bunkyo, Itabashi) and most of Saitama-shi.

### Cited Findings
- All warehouse locations, addresses and coordinates are from the table above — [OSM/Overpass](https://overpass-api.de/); [setu.co.jp](https://setu.co.jp/costco-shoplist-kanto/); [hubmedia](https://hubmedia.co.jp/costco/costco-store/japan-all-costco-stores/)
- **How the distances were estimated (no routing service used, so every figure is an estimate).**
  - **Start point:** each municipality's main residential area is represented by one point, usually its ward or city office or main station (coordinates approximate, from general knowledge).
  - **Straight-line distance:** haversine distance from that point to each warehouse.
  - **Road distance:** straight-line × 1.35.
  - **Drive time:** a range.
    - The low end assumes an average of 30 km/h, with good expressway access and off-peak traffic.
    - The high end assumes 18 km/h from the 23 wards and 22 km/h elsewhere, i.e. surface streets with weekend traffic.
    - Costco car-park queues on weekends can add 15–30+ min; this is not included.

### Inferences
- **Tiers by Costco access (estimated):**
  - Tier A, ≤20 min: Narashino, Chiba Mihama, Hanamigawa, Inage, Funabashi, Inzai, Kisarazu, Sodegaura, Misato, Yashio, Nagareyama, Tsukuba, Kawasaki-ku, Saiwai, Kanazawa-ku, Ota.
  - Tier B, about 20–40 min: Chiba Chuo, Wakaba, Shiroi, Yachiyo, Sakura, Kamagaya, Ichikawa, Urayasu, Matsudo, Kashiwa, Koshigaya, Soka, Machida, Tama, Tachikawa, Fuchu, Inagi, Asao, Nakahara, Kohoku, Aoba, Shinagawa, Adachi, Tsukubamirai, Moriya.
  - Tier C, 45+ min: most central and western 23 wards and most Saitama-shi wards.
- **Saitama-shi.** It is the largest candidate city without a nearby Costco. If the rumoured Saitama-city store ever materialises it would change this, but nothing is announced, so a family choosing Saitama-shi should assume 50–80 min for at least 2026–2027.
- **Chiba choices.** Among the Chiba candidates, Mihama, Hanamigawa, Narashino and Funabashi are effectively next door to 幕張. Inzai and Shiroi are next door to 千葉ニュータウン.
- **Near-ties.** Where the nearest and 2nd-nearest Costco are within about 3 km of road distance, real-world expressway access decides which is faster. Examples: Koto (幕張 via Wangan), Moriya (つくば via Joban), Ichihara (Tateyama Expwy to 木更津), Nerima, Tsuzuki and Yachiyo.

### Gaps
- No routing engine (Google Maps or NAVITIME) was used, so all road distances and times are modelled estimates (×1.35 detour factor, 18–30 km/h). Real times vary with time of day and expressway use. Weekend Costco car-park entry queues are notorious and are not included.
- Each municipality's representative point is approximate (ward or city office, or main station from general knowledge), not population-weighted. Large municipalities can vary by ±10 min internally. Examples: Setagaya west vs east, Chiba Midori-ku (Toke vs Kamatori), Ichihara north vs south, Saitama Nishi-ku.
- Toll costs, e.g. the Tokyo Bay Aqua-Line for 木更津 from Kanagawa or Ota, were not researched.
